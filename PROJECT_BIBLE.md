# 2. Vision

BANKGUARD AI is an AI-powered Risk, Fraud and Regulatory
Intelligence Copilot for Banking and NBFC teams.

It transforms transaction and account activity into:

**Signal → Evidence → Finding → Regulatory Context → Action**

The objective is to reduce the manual effort involved in:

- Fraud investigation
- AML transaction monitoring
- Risk analysis
- Regulatory intelligence
- Investigation documentation
- Audit preparation

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Banking and NBFC teams manage large volumes of:

- Customer transactions
- Account activity
- Fraud alerts
- AML signals
- Risk indicators
- Internal policies
- Regulatory documents
- Investigation records

The current workflow is often fragmented and manual.

Analysts may need to move between multiple systems to:

1. Identify a suspicious signal
2. Investigate the underlying activity
3. Collect supporting evidence
4. Determine the applicable policy
5. Document the finding
6. Prepare an audit or regulatory report

BANKGUARD AI aims to connect these steps into a single
intelligence workflow.

# 5. Core Use Cases

## UC-01 – Risk Monitoring

Identify high-risk customers, accounts and transactions.

## UC-02 – Fraud Detection

Identify suspicious transaction patterns and fraud signals.

## UC-03 – AML Investigation

Identify potential:

- Transaction structuring
- Transaction velocity anomalies
- Beneficiary anomalies
- Geographic anomalies
- Dormant account activation

## UC-04 – Evidence Investigation

Explain why a customer, account or transaction was flagged.

## UC-05 – Regulatory Intelligence

Connect risk findings to relevant policies and regulatory
documents.

## UC-06 – Investigation Report

Generate structured, audit-ready investigation findings.

## UC-07 – Executive Intelligence

Summarize emerging fraud and risk patterns.
